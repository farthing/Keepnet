I fish a lot in Forever, and none of the fishing addons I tried did quite what I wanted, so I made my own. Keepnet is the result: one key to fish with, a record of everything I catch, and a few things I kept wishing I had while standing at the water.

It is a hobby project. If something is broken or you have an idea, tell me (how is at the bottom of this page).

What it does

  • Fishing with one key. It casts when the line is in and reels in when it's out. The same key puts a lure on, eats your fishing food and wears your fishing gear when needed.
  • A history you can search. Every session is kept with its time, casts, catches and gold, separately for each character. Type a fish's name and see which zones you caught it in.
  • The skill every zone asks for. All the zones in one list, coloured by whether your Fishing is enough, with your sessions in each.
  • Trainers and vendors near you. Who teaches Fishing and who sells poles and lures, zone by zone, showing only the ones your faction can use. Click one for a targeting macro and a mark on your map. (New in 1.2.22.)
  • A bobber you can't miss. A mark hangs over your bobber and goes from green to yellow to red as the line's time runs out. It has colour blind colours too, and so does the rest of Keepnet's text.

And the rest:

  • A live panel with your skill, casts, catches, what the catch is worth and gold per hour. You can resize it.
  • Lures bought for you at vendors: you pick the lure, how many to keep, and an amount of gold it must never touch.
  • A second key that swaps your fishing pole and gear on and off.
  • Alerts for 121 rare catches, with a sound for each kind (your own sound files work too).
  • What comes out of trunks, chests, clams and bloated fish is added to the session that caught them.
  • Prices from Auctionator, TradeSkillMaster, Auctioneer, aux or Oribos Exchange.
  • The game's interface can hide while you fish, leaving the panel and a countdown for the line.
  • Several looks: flat, rounded, see-through Glass, the game's own windows, EllesmereUI's, and a Windows 95 one for fun. The window and the panel each have a size slider.
  • A minimap button, and a note when a newer version is out (heard from other Keepnet players; you can turn it off).

How it looks

Equip a fishing pole and the small panel on the left appears and keeps the score while you fish.

The live panel

  • The zone and the skill it asks for. The number in brackets is the Fishing skill that water needs before nothing gets away. Green means you have it, yellow means some fish will get away, red means you can't cast there yet.
  • Your Fishing skill as a bar with numbers.
  • Casts, catches and empty casts for the session.
  • What the catch is worth, and gold per hour, priced from your auction addon and updated every few seconds.
  • What's in use: the lure the key applies, your food and your fishing gear, with a button to switch lure-applying on and off.
  • Everything caught this session, most first, with each item's count and its share of the catch. A long list scrolls with the mouse wheel.
  • Resizable: drag the panel's bottom right corner to make it wider, or to show more catches before the list scrolls.
  • Splash only / Game audio: one button switches between a loud splash with everything else quiet, and your normal sound.
  • A cog in the corner opens the settings.

Change the lure, food or gear without leaving the water

Click the lure, food or gear line on the panel and a list opens under it: every lure or food in your bags, and for gear each slot with the pieces you could wear. Pick one, or leave it on automatic.

One key does the fishing

  • Casts when the line is in, reels in when it's out. Only while a fishing pole is equipped, so the key is yours again the moment you put the pole away.
  • Puts a lure on first when there is none: the best one in your bags, or the one you choose.
  • Eats fishing food and uses other items you list (a coin, a toy) when their buff runs out.
  • Puts on fishing gear from your bags with the pole, and puts your own back afterwards. For each slot you choose: the best you have, a piece you pick, or leave the slot alone.
  • Every cast, lure and reel-in is your own key press. One press, one action.
  • A second key for the gear, if you want one: it puts your fishing pole on, and the fishing gear with it, and on the next press puts back what you held and wore. It works in combat too, and can be a combination such as Ctrl and F.

Sessions and history

A session starts with your first cast and ends after a break or when you move to another zone. Each one keeps its time, casts, catches and gold.

What you open counts too

Open a trunk, a chest, a clam or a bloated fish that you fished up, and what comes out of it, coins included, is added to the session that caught it, even if you open it days later. The Session tab lists those items as "from an opened catch".

Where do I catch it?

  1. Open the History tab.
  2. Type part of a fish's or an item's name in the "Find a fish or item" box.
  3. The list becomes the zones you caught it in. The zone where it made up the largest share of your catch comes first, with how many you caught there, out of how many catches, and in how many sessions.

Empty the box and your sessions are back. That list sorts by any column with a click on its heading, and can be narrowed to one zone.

Every zone, and the skill it asks for

The Zones tab lists every zone with the Fishing skill it asks for, whether you have fished there or not: the zones you have fished first, then the rest from the lowest skill up. Click a zone to see its last 20 sessions with their time, casts, catches, gold and gold per hour. Click a session to open it with everything caught in it.

Trainers and vendors

The "Trainers & vendors" button on the Zones tab swaps the sessions for the people: who teaches Fishing and who sells tackle, in the zone you clicked or in all of them, with where they stand. A Horde character sees Horde and neutral ones, an Alliance character sees Alliance and neutral ones. Click one and Keepnet makes a macro called "KeepnetTarget" that targets them, and marks the spot on your map if you are in their zone. General trade vendors who happen to stock a pole are left out unless you tick "General vendors too". The list comes from Wowhead's data for Forever, so if someone is missing or stands somewhere else, let me know.

Rare catches

Keepnet knows 121 catches that are rare and come from fishing: every trophy fish, The 1 Ring and the fish weapons, Message in a Bottle, the tournament fish, bloated fish, the chests of open water and the named coins. Most of them are plain white or grey items, so item quality alone would never flag them.

  • A sound, a line on screen, a chat line and a highlight on the panel.
  • Tick which kinds you want to hear about.
  • Also alert for any item of a chosen quality, or worth a chosen amount of gold.
  • A different sound for each kind of catch, including sound files of your own.

Lures bought for you

Off until you turn it on, in the Gear & food tab.

  • Buys when it sees them: open a vendor that sells the lure you want and Keepnet buys it. Chat says what was bought and what it cost.
  • You choose which: one of the five basic lures, from a list: Shiny Bauble, Nightcrawlers, Aquadynamic Fish Lens, Bright Baubles, Flesh Eating Worm.
  • You choose how many: it keeps your number in your bags (20 unless you change it): have fewer, and a vendor visit buys what is missing.
  • Aquadynamic Fish Attractor: a tick box of its own buys every one a vendor has, since vendors only ever have a few.
  • The money you keep: set an amount in gold and silver and buying lures never takes you under it. With that much or less, nothing is bought at all.

A bobber you can always see

  • While the line is out, a mark hangs over your bobber: a ring, an arrow pointing down at the bobber, and a middle that tells the time. Green for the first 10 seconds, fading to yellow by 20 and to red by 29.9.
  • From 20 seconds it pulses gently, more and more until the end, so the end of the line shows by more than colour.
  • On the Settings tab, under Bobber mark: switch it on or off, its size, your own colour for the circle and for the arrow, colour blind colours, and the pulse.
  • Colour blind colours for the circle, the arrow and the three timer colours: Protanopia and Deuteranopia (sky blue, yellow, vermillion), Tritanopia (cyan, pink, red), Monochrome (light to dark grey), or "As the game's colour blind mode", which follows the game's own Colorblind Filter. The three colours in use are shown next to the choice, and a circle or arrow colour you pick yourself always wins.
  • It stays when Keepnet hides the game's interface.
  • How it finds your bobber: while the line is out, Keepnet turns on the game's soft target settings for objects, so the game gives your bobber a nameplate for the mark to hang from. Your own settings come back when the line comes in, at combat and at logout. With the whole interface hidden, the game's nameplates are kept showing so the mark can stay; switch the mark off and they hide with the interface as before.

Out of your way

  • Hide the game's interface while fishing: everything except the live panel, or just the parts you tick. It comes back by itself when you move, when combat starts, or when something needs an answer.
  • The line's time and what it brought up: with the interface hidden, a strip under the panel shows a bar running down with the seconds the line has left, and for a few seconds what each cast brought up.
  • Set how solid the panel's background is, down to just its text over the game, and how big the panel is.

Prices

Gold figures come from the auction addon you choose: Auctionator, TradeSkillMaster, Auctioneer, aux or Oribos Exchange. Grey items, items that bind when picked up and anything without an auction price count at the vendor price. An item with no price at all is left out of the total and shown as "without price", never counted as zero.

New versions

Keepnet tells you when a newer version is out: a line in chat after you log in, a line on the live panel when your pole goes on, and a line in Settings.

  • How it knows. An addon cannot reach the internet, so copies of Keepnet tell each other their version in hidden messages. They do it on a chat channel of Keepnet's own, called KeepersNet, which Keepnet joins by itself a few seconds after you log in, and to your guild and your party or raid.
  • Nothing shows in chat. The channel is on none of your chat windows and none of its lines are printed. It does take one of your chat channel places, and it is gone again when you log out.
  • Hard to fool. A newer version is only believed once two different players have announced it.
  • Turning it off. Settings, "New versions", untick "Tell me when a newer Keepnet is out". Keepnet then leaves the channel, tells nobody its version and listens to nobody.
  • It works between copies of 1.0.0 or newer.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/kn or /keepnetOpen the window
/kn newEnd the session; a new one starts with your next cast
/kn panelHide or show the live panel
/kn uiHide the game's interface, or bring it back
/kn demoShow sample sessions, to try the screens before you have a history

Something not working?

  • Open Keepnet, go to the Settings tab and click Report a problem. A window opens with a log of what Keepnet has been doing, and the steps to send it.
  • Click Select all, press Ctrl+C, then paste it into a new issue at github.com/farthing/Keepnet/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). Say what you expected and what happened instead.
  • The log holds only Keepnet's own steps: no chat and no other players. Your character names are taken out before it is shown. It does include your gold where Keepnet weighed up a purchase.
  • Nothing is sent by itself: an addon can't reach the internet. The log leaves your game only when you copy it out.

Got an idea?

Feature suggestions go to the same place: open a new issue at github.com/farthing/Keepnet/issues and choose "Suggest a feature".

Good to know

  • Disable other one-key fishing addons while you use Keepnet: they bind the same key and change the same game settings.
  • Your own sound settings and key come back when the pole comes off.
  • Lures and fishing food are recognised from their English tooltips.
  • Auction addons are optional. Without one, everything counts at vendor prices.

How this was made

I'm not a programmer or a developer. If I could write this myself, I would. Generative AI is what makes it possible now: Keepnet is written with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. I decide what it should do, try every change in the game, and nothing goes up that I haven't played with myself. This page was written with its help too.
